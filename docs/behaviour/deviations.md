# Deliberate deviations from the original

Places where the remake intentionally differs from the original game. Each row names the evidence level of the
original behaviour; "unverified" means no capture or debug-build walk backs it.

| Id | Area | Original (evidence) | Remake | Reason | Issue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DEV-1 | Skirmish selection | Only own objects, plus allied community property in a multiplayer tactical game, can be selected (debug-build walk of the selection admission in the FoC debug build via GhidrAssist; no capture, no committed walk artifact). Whether skirmish against AI counts as that mode, and the panel contents, are unverified by capture. | A click on an allied structure inspects it: health card, no commands, build menu or abilities; viewer-local, no net message. Own and enemy objects unchanged. An allied click replaces your current selection with the inspect card; reselect your units afterwards. Allied build pads and community-property stations keep their existing shared control ((legacy EAWR-1571), WBP-08/09, WSU-16: selectable with build UI); DEV-1 covers the other allied structures. | Owner decision 2026-10-24: the mechanic never made sense. | (legacy EAWR-4919) |
| DEV-2 | Particle presentation: one catch-up pass where the difference is under half a pixel (D-ss) | Retail runs one particle update per rendered frame; emitters consume the elapsed time of the pose just supplied and births are placed from the emitter's motion over that interval (PS-42, PS-10; behaviour docs). | In a frame that owes two or more ticks, a ship whose emitters would differ by less than 0.5 px on screen (predicted from the two pose snapshots and the current camera; back at 0.25 px) takes one pass at the drawn pose instead of a historical pass plus the drawn pass; culled ships always take one. Births are placed along the straight chord between the last and the new pose, so the difference is the path's deviation from that chord. Simulation and per-tick hashes are identical. Pixels of fire and smoke clusters in crowded fleets differ: on the RaW60 build host pair at 2 ticks/frame 21 479 of 921 600 pixels (2.33%) of the final frame, mean 0.066, max channel 205, 34 pixels >= 128; the threshold bounds the origin path error, not this pixel difference. The owner watched a build host RaW60 off/on clip (2 ticks/frame, ticks 3400-3600, about 2.4% of pixels differ per frame, sim hashes identical) on 2026-11-01 at 09:05 and accepted it. Not covered: close-up fighter trails, other ticks per frame. | Removes most of the historical pass in frames below 30 fps. | (legacy EAWR-5594) |
| DEV-3 | Galactic route cost width | Retail stores the route cost in 16 bits, so it overflows at 65 000+ route units (evidence level as recorded on (legacy EAWR-4323, EAWR-4312); no new capture). | Routes are computed exactly with 64-bit cost (shipped in c6c16e11); no overflow. No replay or checkpoint change. The documented ceiling stays: galaxies of more than 1000 planets are still capped. | Owner decision 2026-11-01 (legacy EAWR-4323): keep the wider number. | (legacy EAWR-4323, EAWR-4312) |
| DEV-4 | Galactic hero bar: live and scheduled entry of one type | Retail appends a scheduled-spawn entry for every named local hero in a planet spawn list without checking the live scan, so a type present in both would take two slots (debug-build hero-bar walk via GhidrAssist, 2026-11-01; no capture of the overlap, which needs a hero both alive and scheduled). | One portrait per type: the live entry wins and the scheduled one is dropped. Presentation only; no sim, hash or replay change. | A named hero is unique, so the overlap is a transient oddity and a duplicate portrait has no value. | (legacy EAWR-3899) |
| DEV-5 | Galactic story player lookup in co-op | `Find_Player("local")` uses the local machine's player slot (WST-91; debug-build walk). Co-op on single-player campaign entries is not a retail mode. | In a networked campaign with single-player rules and one shared human faction, the lookup returns that agreed faction on every peer. Other networked campaigns do not expose a per-peer local player. | A per-machine value would diverge in lockstep; the shared faction lets co-op story scripts use a deterministic player. | (legacy EAWR-5626) |
