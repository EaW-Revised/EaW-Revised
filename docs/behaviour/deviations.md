Places where the remake intentionally differs from the original game. Each row names the evidence level of the
original behaviour; "unverified" means no capture or debug-build walk backs it.

| DEV-2 | Particle presentation: one catch-up pass where the difference is under half a pixel (D-ss) | Retail runs one particle update per rendered frame; emitters consume the elapsed time of the pose just supplied and births are placed from the emitter's motion over that interval (PS-42, PS-10; behaviour docs). | In a frame that owes two or more ticks, a ship whose emitters would differ by less than 0.5 px on screen (predicted from the two pose snapshots and the current camera; back at 0.25 px) takes one pass at the drawn pose instead of a historical pass plus the drawn pass; culled ships always take one. Births are placed along the straight chord between the last and the new pose, so the difference is the path's deviation from that chord. Simulation and per-tick hashes are identical. Pixels of fire and smoke clusters in crowded fleets differ: on the RaW60 build host pair at 2 ticks/frame 21 479 of 921 600 pixels (2.33%) of the final frame, mean 0.066, max channel 205, 34 pixels >= 128; the threshold bounds the origin path error, not this pixel difference. The owner watched a build host RaW60 off/on clip (2 ticks/frame, ticks 3400-3600, about 2.4% of pixels differ per frame, sim hashes identical) on 2026-11-01 at 09:05 and accepted it. Not covered: close-up fighter trails, other ticks per frame. | Removes most of the historical pass in frames below 30 fps. | (legacy EAWR-5594) |

| DEV-8 | Galactic AI decision timing | The AI's world actions (production, fleet orders, invasions) and its bookkeeping (script variables, pay-as-you-go billing decisions, story-plot commands) take effect as the AI performs its work (timing evidence unverified by capture). | The AI decides in windows of 15 ticks from the world as it was when the window began, plus the AI's own world actions from earlier windows that have not applied yet. Each decision's world action applies at its own apply tick, 30 to 44 ticks (one to one and a half seconds at normal speed) after that snapshot (the batch schedule of the async AI); each window owns fixed entity and invasion identity blocks reserved when it begins. World events for the AI (production outcomes, movement events, unit lifecycle changes, battle notices) reach it at its next window start, in the order they happened. That includes the story scripts' Spawn_Unit and Prevent_AI_Usage: the new object enters the world at once and its AI owner's free store at that window start, and a Prevent_AI_Usage reaches the AI's free store then too; the script's answer is decided from the world immediately: a selected AI owner is accepted even before its pool is initialized. The world retains both actor facts and seeds them into a later pool. What the world delivers while one of the AI's slices applies (loss notices, credits, bills, and the consequence of a hop or purchase it refuses, which finishes the movement blocks that were open when the world refused (a later movement of the same fleet stays open) or fails its purchase row) waits in the same queue and reaches the AI at the next window start, behind the notices of the steps before it; on a window tick the due slices apply first and the queue is cut after them, so the window sees them (the AI writes its own state inside its window only). Income, windfalls and pay-as-you-go bills reach its budget the same way; between windows a bill is decided against the budget the AI published at its last apply tick plus the income, windfalls and bills since (at most about 1 s old), and the AI books that recorded outcome without deciding again (a charge for a goal it has removed meanwhile goes to its dead budget). The story-plot edits of the AI's plan scripts (Get_Story_Plot) take effect at the window's apply tick, after that window's world actions; an edit the plot engine refuses is reported at that tick. Runtime notes the AI raises during a window (a failed battle notification, a retired AI provider, its plan scripts' own notes) are reported at that tick too. Script variables (GlobalValue) are split: story scripts read and write their own store at once; the AI's plan scripts see their own writes at once and the story's from their next window, and the AI's writes reach the story's store at the window's apply tick (on the same key the AI's later write wins). The AI's remaining bookkeeping is still written during the window; the remaining async-lane steps move it to the window's first apply tick. Human player actions are unaffected. | Owner approved 2026-11-04 (restated 2026-11-07) to let AI work run without blocking the simulation. | AI lane work (legacy EAWR-5806) |
