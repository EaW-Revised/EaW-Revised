# Front end shell: the main menu and the screens it leads to

What the viewer shows before a battle: the main menu, the single-player and multiplayer menus, and how
the player moves between them and the screens built elsewhere (the skirmish setup screen, the settings
screen). Evidence: the effective FoC dialog resources, skin XML, game constants, music and SFX event
files and text database, and the debug build for each menu's creation and button handling. The
engine-free model is `include/eawr/presentation/ui/front_end.hpp` (cases in `tests/ui/front_end_tests.cpp`);
the viewer builds and draws it in `apps/viewer/src/front_end_mode.cpp` and hosts it in
`viewer_host_*.cpp` (`--eawr-front-end`). The viewer's GPU case is
`tests/presentation/renderer/test_front_end.py`. The retail captures of the main menu and the single-player menu (1280 by 720) back FES-04, FES-06 and FES-08.

The menus and the skirmish setup screen are laid out on a fixed 1280 by 720 canvas that one node transform
scales to the window (letterboxed, uniform). Their text is rasterised at the scale it is drawn at (Godot's
per-node oversampling on the scaled root, kept current for parts shown after a resize), not at the canvas size
and stretched, so a 1440p window is as sharp as 720p (legacy EAWR-3176). The GPU case
`test_text_is_as_sharp_at_a_large_window_as_at_1280x720` measures it. The skin textures are already cut at texel
resolution and resampled once. At a fractional scale a glyph still lands between two pixels where the canvas
position times the scale is fractional; the root's own origin is a whole pixel.

## Not in this build (fidelity list)

- The demo battle behind the menu (`Main_Menu_Demo_Attract_Mode`, `Demo_Attract_Maps`, the start timeout and
  cycle delay) and the version line retail draws in its debug scene.
- The credits movie, the tutorial selection and the story campaign selection: their buttons are shown disabled.
- The custom campaign staging's difficulty levels other than normal and its advanced options page (credits, tech
  levels, auto-resolve): shown disabled until the galactic mode applies them; the profile's last campaign and the
  preview's radar blip icon are not applied (the preview draws plain squares on the shared galaxy backdrop).
- Retail stretches the dialogs' x axis (at 1280 wide: 1.25) where the shell keeps the project's uniform scale (UI-L3, aspect-correct): the menus are narrower than in the retail capture and the backdrop's baked logo is not under the button it belongs to.
- The land skirmish choice: the setup screen offers space maps only.
