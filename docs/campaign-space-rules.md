# Campaign space battle rules

`campaign::apply_space_rules` decorates a prepared conflict without filesystem reads or galaxy mutation. The recorded `CampaignBattleSetup` holds both accounts, persistent reserve identities, restrictions, retained planet upgrades, initial population registration and extra campaign victory conditions. `bind_space_rules` reconstructs that policy for replay and checkpoint loading.

WGT-26 selects `Tactical_Buildable_Objects_Campaign` and `Tactical_Build_Cost_Campaign`; multiplayer menus remain independent. Campaign pad construction applies the qualified corruption price multiplier. Existing automatic-upgrade capability gates still apply. WGT-27 keeps galactic credits and tech, caps credit changes by `Credit_Cap_Per_Planet * max(1, owned planets)`, and uses the faction's space population cap. WPR-02 does not make tactical production admission read tech, so this adapter does not invent an additional tech gate.

WGA-53 transfers only explicitly supplied budget categories. A known-empty stock category set uses `budgets_known`; absent qualification refuses the battle. WGA-67..70 require the producer to export each galactic category's current available cash and tactical allowance after template activation, initial allocation and live spending. The S4 galactic AI service owns that producer; this adapter does not substitute tactical template budgets or static percentages. Commented-out allowances are never enabled, while a selected intervention template may explicitly supply its authored allowance. Replay opcode 29 carries WGA-54 additional credit release, including negative requests for all remaining strategic credits. The ordered return DTO restores switched balances and exports qualified persistent non-level-up upgrade objects; the campaign return owner consumes the DTO once.

Additional release tracks only credits admitted within the current credit-cap headroom. A partial or blocked release leaves the remainder in the saved strategic pool, including a negative release-all request; later spending can make room for another transfer. WGA-54 sources the transfer/restoration accounting, not discarding a cap-blocked portion.

WGT-28 reserve tokens are the persistent root identities. A deployment consumes that exact token once, retains its ID, applies side facing and restriction checks, and counts initial/deployed population independently of the production menu. Qualified loaded transports preserve the original purchase type and recursive passenger identities; passengers receive no independent pool offer or replacement ID. Generated craft use the shared allocator. Upgrade bonuses apply even when a persistent reserve ID is below that allocator's frontier.

Human admission accepts a persistent token only through a trusted `CampaignReserveAdmission` value supplied by the active campaign child owner. It matches the bound player's recorded reserve identity, type, ownership and restriction against the completed economy pool. Default standalone, skirmish and LAN admission rejects nonzero tokens; an inactive context, foreign player, substituted type or consumed token also refuses. Additional credit release remains engine-only. The return owner passes this context through both human command admission gates; the incoming command cannot author it.

WGT-29 always installs all-enemy-units victory with the sourced 210-frame countdown. Explicit leader IDs and the optional station-destruction condition are evaluated at the existing ordered lifecycle hook. No lobby timer or free starting-force template is admitted. WGT-31 story objectives remain the S5 interface.

WGT-33 binds campaign perception, the defender's player ID and current base level into the ordinary space AI. `script::foc::prepare_session` returns a scheduler and a callback sharing one host; the callback binds the existing campaign child after creation or restoration. It creates no second tactical owner. The retreat owner supplies both `AiSetup::campaign_can_retreat` (a pure qualification against the completed snapshot) and `campaign_retreat_order` (the ordinary authoritative retreat order). Qualified stock `TaskForce.Withdraw_Units` stages that order through the deferred script command route; missing callbacks or standalone mode remain inert. Galactic perception and budget services remain explicit campaign inputs.

The presentation-only `space_briefing.hpp` contract implements SND-41/49 and WBF-09. Briefing waits for frame 300, cinematic completion and an empty speech queue, retains its one-shot state before resolving speech, scans authored OR rows before AND rows, and treats C as an exclusion. A qualifying local attacking-space intro attempts the cinematic; network, disabled or delayed-arrival cases use the ordinary fade. The presentation owner resolves faction speech/movie content and retains the briefing state.

Replay extension 10 (campaign body version 3) and extension 11 (carrier body version 1) record campaign policy including reserve attachments and initial carrier trees respectively. Carrier metadata is bounded by the existing header limit, 32 nesting levels and the tactical object limit; identities, parents, spatial flags and allocator ownership are validated. Child checkpoint section 11 is version 8 (each spawner also retains the ledger of its single-ship launches, WSL-16a, so skirmish checkpoint bytes carry one extra empty length prefix per spawner while the state hash is unchanged); its raw child-state domain is 9 and includes each player's collected tactical salvage tally and retained squadron attack attachments. Older project saves need no compatibility reader before 1.0.

`galactic_space_rules` proves exact credits and upgrades, persistent deployment and refusal, extra victory conditions, retained bonuses, briefing gates and replay/checkpoint continuation across 1/2/4/8 workers. `galactic_space_rules_stock` loads installed FoC campaign menus/costs and prepares/services the ordinary campaign AI without relying on regenerated game pins.
