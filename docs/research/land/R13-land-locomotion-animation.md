# R13: land locomotion and animation

This read-only research covers infantry member animation, ordinary ground
movement animation, deployment, terrain posing and moving squad spacing. It
informs the land sandbox's unit tables (L2), local movement (L5), live view (L6)
and land movement integration (legacy EAWR-1451), within the ground-combat
architecture programme (legacy EAWR-3776). It changes no runtime code or tag status.

Evidence is the debug build, inspected read-only on 2026-10-22. Opaque ER13
identifiers map to retained queries and inspection results in ignored
`out/research/gc-land-r13/`. Symbol matches alone establish no rule. There was
no retail run, capture, frame-order experiment or asset-specific visual verdict.
Public animation names below are the clip suffixes from
[unit animation](../../behaviour/unit-animation.md) UA-01/02, rather than private
binary identifiers. In particular, `MOVE` is the ordinary faster cycle,
`WALKMOVE` the slower cycle, and `WALK` the deployed-walking cycle; these are
different clip kinds.

[Land pathing](../../behaviour/walks/land-pathing.md) WLPA-26..32 already owns
slot arrangement and following; U-WLPA-3 includes their still-open steering and
animation details. [Land abilities](../../behaviour/walks/land-abilities.md)
WLAB-12..17 owns spread out, sprint, stim pack, jet pack and the deploy request.
R13 narrows U-WLPA-3 and the previously unnamed R13 presentation gap; no row
claims to close that entire movement item. The remaining R13 portions receive
U-LR13 identifiers below. Bridge/crowd outcomes remain U-WLPA-2/6 and U-LC-3.

For schedule questions, consult the
[frame service schedule](../../behaviour/frame-service-schedule.md)
FS-22/37..39, FC-44/46/69 and FD-64, then the land frame-map programme. The
sequence of rules here is explanatory, not a proposed frame schedule. Exact
service admission, first movement and same-frame animation/weapon dependencies
remain U-WLPA-1 and U-LR13-06.

## Data and simulation boundary

The animation policy uses the loaded model's clip inventory and authored type
settings. UA-01/02 still owns model-stem overrides, contiguous clip variants and
missing-animation behavior. Do not infer a fixed infantry/walker model list from
the examples or give every vehicle a clip that its assets lack.

The verified readers include `Movement_Animation_Speed`, `Walk_Animation_Speed`,
`Crouch_Animation_Speed`, `WalkAnimationCutoff`, `Walk_Transition`,
`Stopped_Rate_Of_Turn`, `Rotation_Animation_Speed`, `Has_Pre_Turn_Anim`,
`Deployed_Walk_Speed`, `Deployed_Walk_Anim_Speed`, `Deployment_Anim_Rate`,
`Is_Stationary_When_Attacking`, `Max_Dist_Walk_While_Deployed`,
`Overall_Length`, `Overall_Width`, `MovementClass`,
`InfantryTurnBlendTime`, `TeamMoveBlendTime`, `TeamCrouchMoveBlendTime`,
`IdleWalkBlendTime` and `CrouchIdleWalkBlendTime`. Authored fields remain distinct;
the object-definition and game-constant catalogs identify their data interfaces.
This walk does not apply any of them in runtime or change registry coverage.
The complete consumers of nearby `Locomotor_Has_Animation_Priority`, movement
overrides and special-ability animation priorities require qualification below;
recognizing a tag is not proof that every locomotor reads it. The ordinary
ground policy is not automatically the bike, flying or jet-pack policy. Ground
aircraft have their own [R6 flight research](R6-air-over-land.md).

For the remake, root positions, facing, member slot assignment and
ability/deployment state are simulation-owned inputs. Clip choices and progress
that no simulation rule consumes can remain presentation-owned. Animation kind
and completion used by locomotion must be represented explicitly in authoritative
movement state. Do not make
headless motion depend on the viewer having drawn a frame. The eventual
implementation keeps partitioned per-member work and ordered commits; this
research does not prescribe a serial retail update loop.
