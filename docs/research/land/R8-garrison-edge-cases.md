# R8: garrison edge cases

## Scope and evidence

Read-only research on 2026-10-22 for the ground-combat architecture programme
(legacy EAWR-3776) and land garrison integration (legacy EAWR-1455). Comparison base:
`3b1c4134aac37b9094d5e53167b5694352cb24f7`. This extends
[land combat](../../behaviour/walks/land-combat.md), LC-37..41 and U-LC-4, and
[land pathing](../../behaviour/walks/land-pathing.md), WLPA-43/48..50 and U-WLPA-5.
It covers physical occupants of structures and transports; the separately named
player garrison roster is distinguished below.

Evidence is the **debug build**, effective XML and inherited sourced rules.
ER8 identifiers name successful private inspection groups; DR8-01 identifies the
effective XML tag/provenance snapshot. The evidence map, raw inspections and
receipts remain in ignored `out/research/gc-land-r8/`. No retail capture, game-file
change or implementation is claimed. Authored values are not engine limits or
defaults for mods. Symbol matches alone are not evidence of behavior.

The [shared frame schedule](../../behaviour/frame-service-schedule.md) and
[frame-order walk](../../behaviour/walks/frame-order.md) own service placement.
R1 owns land cadence, first service and same-frame conflicts. The transitions
below describe an admitted operation, not a new scheduling rule. Neither older
U-item is closed in full: each row states the portion it settles, with the
remaining seams and exact follow-up captures listed below.

## Programme handoff

L1 retains terrain, geometry and zone inputs. L2 loads masks, slot/value data,
behavior selection and model-bone references without stock lists or bounds.
L4/L5 supply target-specific approach/contact admission, nearest-open policy,
completion callbacks and deploy/hover/outward movement. L6 presents request
feedback separately from committed containment, fade and qualified firing poses.
Physical garrison integration (legacy EAWR-1455) owns identity, pending versus occupied
accounting, entry/exit states and terminal cleanup. Combat (legacy EAWR-1454) consumes
the firing policy; player reinforcement work (legacy EAWR-1457) consumes roster upgrades.

Use partitioned immutable proposals and ordered commits for competing unit/host
operations, with equality on 1/2/4/8 workers. This is the project's implementation
contract, not a claim about retail threading. U-LC-4 and U-WLPA-5 remain partially
open at the explicit seams above; a researched normal path is not certification
of destruction, ownership, obstruction or checkpoint behavior.
