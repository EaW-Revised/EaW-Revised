# R6: aircraft over land and bombing-run flight

## Scope and evidence

This walk supplies the flight boundary for land support (legacy EAWR-1459) and the
ground-combat architecture programme (legacy EAWR-3776). It distinguishes ordinary
land aircraft from the aircraft created for a bombing run. It extends
[land combat](../../behaviour/walks/land-combat.md), especially LC-27 and
LC-58..63, and [land pathing](../../behaviour/walks/land-pathing.md). It does not
reopen orbital bombardment, transport unloading, space-member flight or weapon
damage arithmetic.

Evidence is read-only inspection of the **debug build**, effective XML, and the
existing sourced walks, on 2026-10-22. ER6 evidence IDs identify private inspections;
DR6 IDs identify data observations. Their map, raw results and data provenance stay
in ignored `out/research/gc-land-r6/`. No retail capture or implementation was made.
Authored examples below are neither engine defaults nor supported-value limits.

The [frame-service schedule](../../behaviour/frame-service-schedule.md) explicitly
excludes land bombing runs from live space-member flight. R1 owns land service
placement, admission, cadence and same-frame dependencies. The state transitions
below describe what an admitted operation does; they do not establish which object
or manager acts first. U-WLPA-1 and U-LC-6 remain open for that programme.

## Data boundary

DR6-01 finds authored `FLYING_LOCOMOTOR` carriers in ground-vehicle and cinematic
transport/unique definitions, as well as the dedicated `LANDBOMBER_LOCOMOTOR`
strike definitions. This is an input audit, not a complete inherited-type census or
an engine whitelist. Examples: the ordinary speeder authors `Hover_Offset` 50,
`Prepare_Strafe_Height` 150 and `MaxFacingLookAheadFrames` 5; the checked strike
types author 100, 250 and 30 respectively. The strike types also author `Air`,
`BombingRun`, ordinary weapon/projectile settings, health and shields. Preserve
the data references and ordinary damage path; do not implement a strike as an
invulnerable visual followed by a scripted area-damage rectangle.

The verified readers above cover clearance, turn/lift/roll/bank, model scale,
target distance in the strike stopped branch, ordinary no-target `Min_Speed`,
`Strafe_Distance`, and facing lookahead. Nearby authored
`Max_Thrust`, `Air_Vehicle_Turret_Target`, `Layer_Z_Adjust`, projectile
pulse/recharge settings and reveal range are not all qualified by those readers.
Their presence does not establish use by both locomotors. Shared weapon, reveal,
shield and weather rules remain in their existing walks; the air-specific targeting
consumer and any remaining movement readers need the trace/capture below. Registry
statuses are unchanged because this research applies no new runtime tags.

LC-58/59 retain standalone/campaign bomber selection and cooldown arithmetic;
LC-62/63 retain fog, shield and prevention-circle validation. Flight evidence does
not relax those rules, establish repeated-use cooldown behavior, or settle orbital
support. The cleanup inspection contains a deadline writer for the R1 schedule
walk to qualify; it is not a claim about first availability or same-frame reuse.

## Programme handoff

P1 L1 supplies height, water, bounds and the separately qualified obstacle/tracking
inputs. L2 keeps authored behavior, traversal class, category and container identity
independent. L4/L5 expose air path and flight seams; they must not silently force
aircraft through infantry occupancy or reuse space-member dogfight flight. L6
renders committed aircraft/markers and the qualified display-facing projection.
P4 land support (legacy EAWR-1459) owns request validation, per-player execution,
per-aircraft marker/state identity and terminal cleanup. Its implementation should
use partitioned per-aircraft proposals and ordered creation/target/deletion commits;
that is a project architecture decision, not a claim that retail uses those phases.

This walk decides the normal creation/route/clearance/lifetime boundary. The named
seams remain explicit acceptance work, with scheduling handed to R1 and runtime
capture deferred as requested. It does not certify a finished land-support feature
or close the broader pathing, targeting, frame-order or campaign-return questions.
