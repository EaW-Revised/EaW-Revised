# R10: land AI threat zones and plan discovery

## Scope and evidence

This walk narrows U-WLAI-2/3 in [land tactical AI](../../behaviour/walks/land-ai.md):
how land regions consume threat, and how a land goal can select a plan whose
script is outside the land directory. It supplies the land AI host and
perception packages (legacy EAWR-3207, EAWR-3208), strategy integration (legacy EAWR-3210)
and the ground-combat architecture programme (legacy EAWR-3776). It changes no
runtime code or tag-registry status.

Evidence is read-only inspection of the **debug build**, effective XML/Lua,
and the existing sourced rules on 2026-10-22. ER10 evidence IDs identify private
binary inspections; DR10 IDs identify authored input audits. Raw outputs,
symbols, addresses, input hashes and the evidence map stay in ignored
`out/research/gc-land-r10/`. No retail capture was run. Stock values and script
names describe the inspected fixture, not engine limits or a runtime whitelist.

The [frame-service schedule](../../behaviour/frame-service-schedule.md), FS-17
and FC-37, remains the shared schedule reference. R1 owns land admission,
cadence, reset and same-frame dependencies under U-WLAI-1. The operations below
describe admitted work; their order in this note does not establish frame order.

## Data and implementation boundary

DR10-01 audits the effective AI scripts recursively, finding 53 land-directory
definitions and the retreat declaration outside that directory. This is an input
inventory, not a load limit. DR10-02 checks the three playable player records and
their selected land template. Runtime discovery must preserve mounted content
under the logical search roots and follow selected player/template/goal/script
references, including mod overrides. The stock names above explain the admission
path; the 53-file inventory is not a runtime whitelist.

DR10-05 records effective `AI_FogCellsPerThreatCell` 4,
`AI_LandThreatLookAheadTime` 0 and `AI_LandAreaThreatScaleFactor` 1.
`AI_LandEvaluatorRegionSize` is authored as 2000, but the inspected hint-zone
consumer does not use it to create a square. Its producer-side role is not
established here. `AI_LandThreatRangeCap` has a verified reader; its default is
not inferred from the largest authored weapon range. Nearby
`AI_LandThreatDistanceFactor` 0.5 and `AI_LandThreatTurnRateFactor` 10 require
their actual cost readers before they can justify a distance/turn formula.

The land constants remain marked `land-or-galactic` in the registry; this research
does not apply a tag in runtime code. Company combat power, hardpoints,
company movement masks, company/team expansion and `Has_Land_Evaluator` retain their
existing combat, pathing and perception contracts. A target evaluator and a
threat-grid entry are separate admissions; one must not imply the other.

P3 should prepare copied threat entries, mapped zone footprints and route facts
in disjoint work, then publish shared aggregates in deterministic order. Equal
1/2/4/8-worker results and bounded work are remake requirements, not claims about
retail threading. L4 physical land paths and P3 AI zone routes may share topology
inputs without becoming the same search. L6 may display diagnostic footprints;
no viewer or HUD behavior is decided by this walk.
