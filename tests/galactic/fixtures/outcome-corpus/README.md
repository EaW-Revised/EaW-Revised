# Auto-resolve outcome corpus

`space-loss.eawr-gcreplay` is a synthetic two-player galaxy with one trusted result at the first
completed-tick return boundary. One invading member dies with a defender attribution; both
fleets retain their other members and the political owner is unchanged (WAR-45/54/59/62).
The result uses original types for its record, without tactical health or an invented credit award.

The outcome contract runs this input on 1, 2, 4 and 8 workers, compares the full returned snapshot
with a live selected outcome, and round-trips its applied prefix through a checkpoint. Existing
corpus pins are untouched. Land and container plans, required control/retreat adapters, failed
lethal delivery, runtime ring wrap, and result decoding have separate synthetic contracts.
